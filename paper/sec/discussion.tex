\section{Conclusion}
The effect generalizes: the same three controls separate a composition gap from a constructed fill on WorldFloods and on C2S-MS Floods, so it is not a result of one checkpoint or one dataset.
A larger corpus is informative only when it reports the same three controls, composition, same-tile, and aggregator; the two datasets show the controls, not a size effect.
On WorldFloods the frozen UNet unmatched gap is 39.3, the water-fraction gap is 8.9, and the NDVI gap is 1.4, with a hard paste of 5.6 [3.0, 8.5] on 32 scenes and a test scene mean of 3.8 [1.5, 6.9].
On C2S-MS Floods, frozen Prithvi has composition gaps that exclude zero (unmatched 27.8 [15.3, 39.0], water-fraction 21.1 [10.9, 32.2], NDVI 25.4 [13.9, 36.1], 16 events) and a same-tile paired contrast that includes zero.
Each network is only the frozen checkpoint under that test.
The fill stays a constructed fill on clear tiles, scored on unpainted pixels.
It is not natural cloud.
A third collection was inconclusive and is omitted.
No checkpoint was trained, and the audit adds no model-improvement claim.

\section{Interpretation and limits}
Composition adjustment changes the estimand from an unmatched clear--cloudy comparison to a matched comparison.
A constructed-fill residual is a same-tile intervention, scored on unpainted pixels.
The non-cloud placebo separates a cloud-spectrum contrast from sensitivity to constant replacement.
Where the placebo drop is the larger of the two, the comparison supplies no model ranking.

Aggregation is part of the claim.
An unweighted scene mean, a pixel pool, and an event-weighted mean answer different questions.
The corrected-rule activation follow-up reports uncertainty for a retained stored point.
The original printed point and its original event-weighted alternative stay as printed.
C2S-MS results use their own event-folder units and exclusions, and WorldFloods keeps its own sample counts.

No independent flood labels are invented for unknown JRC history.
The original WorldFloods rasters are currently unavailable in the fresh runtime, so the optional real-tile qualitative paste illustration stays absent, and no other imagery stands in for those rasters.
The source and result records support numerical reproduction.
These controls leave natural-cloud causality, thin-cloud recognition, and a generally improved model unestablished.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The repository retains frozen result JSON, locked rules, object-level input checksums, model revision and checksum, the exact inference environment, the label-coverage census, per-window confusion counts, and all event exclusions.
The C2S-MS result files identify that dataset and DOI and stay outside every WorldFloods contrast sample.
