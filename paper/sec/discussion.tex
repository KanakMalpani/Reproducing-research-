\section{Interpretation and limits}
Composition adjustment changes the estimand; it does not certify that every remaining difference is caused by cloud.
Likewise, a fill can perturb context outside its mask, so a constructed-fill residual is neither a physical-cloud mechanism nor a measurement of hidden flood extent.
The non-cloud placebo is necessary to distinguish a cloud-spectrum-specific contrast from sensitivity to constant replacement.
A small cloud-fill drop alone cannot rank models when the placebo is larger.

Aggregation is part of the claim.
An unweighted scene mean, a pixel pool and an event-weighted mean answer different questions.
The corrected-rule activation follow-up changes uncertainty for a retained stored point; it does not overwrite the original printed point or its original event-weighted alternative.
C2S-MS results use their own event-folder units and exclusions, never WorldFloods sample counts.

No independent flood labels are invented for unknown JRC history.
The original WorldFloods rasters are currently unavailable in the fresh runtime, so the optional real-tile qualitative paste illustration is not substituted with another dataset or synthetic imagery.
The source and result records support numerical reproduction, but a claim of natural-cloud causality, thin-cloud recognition or a generally improved model is not established by these controls alone.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
The repository retains frozen result JSON, locked rules, object-level input checksums, model revision/checksum, exact inference environment, label-coverage census, per-window confusion counts and all event exclusions.
New C2S-MS and SPARCS result files explicitly identify the dataset and DOI and are kept outside every WorldFloods contrast sample.
