\paragraph{Aggregator and coverage.}
The primary point remains the unweighted event-folder scene mean, rather than the pixel pool.
Secondary pixel-pooled composition gaps are 35.98, 23.58 and 28.18 points respectively, demonstrating the numerical effect of aggregation.
As a descriptive accuracy summary, the event-folder mean water IoU is 0.6018354474856003 [0.5074719414119634, 0.688569051715337] on 18 events; secondary pixel-pooled IoU is 0.6436905374529545.
These are accuracy summaries, not clear--cloudy gaps or a model-improvement claim.
The chip census contains 38,200,265 image-valid cloud pixels with known water labels and no water-label no-data pixels in that group; shadows are excluded even when water labels exist.
Full-precision estimates, all event-level confusions, label coverage, matching pairs and exclusions are retained in the new C2S-MS JSON files.
